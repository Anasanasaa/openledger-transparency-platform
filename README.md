# 🚀 OpenLedger — Public Transparency Platform  
*(ET GenAI Hackathon Submission)*

---

## 📌 Overview
OpenLedger is a transparency-focused platform that makes public fundraising accountable by showing how funds are collected and how they are used.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement
During medical emergencies, education support, disaster relief, or community fundraising, donations are often collected by individuals in private accounts.  

Donors lack visibility into:
- Total amount received  
- Where the money is spent  
- Remaining balance  
- Proof of expenses  

This lack of transparency creates:
- Fraud risks  
- Mistrust  
- Hesitation in donating  

---

## 💡 Solution
OpenLedger provides a **public digital ledger** for every campaign where:
- Donations are recorded  
- Expenses are tracked  
- Data is visible to everyone in one place  

👉 OpenLedger does **NOT hold funds**  
👉 It ensures **public accountability and transparency**

---

## 💳 Payment Flow (Prototype Safe Version)
In this prototype:
- Donations are simulated within the platform UI  
- Each donation is recorded with amount and timestamp  
- Total raised updates in real-time  
- Donor name can be optional (anonymous support)  

Future scope includes:
- UPI / Payment Gateway integration  
- Direct transfer to fundraiser accounts  

---

## ✨ Key Features
1. Campaign Creation — title, purpose, beneficiary, goal  
2. Public Campaign Page — total raised, donors, progress bar  
3. Transparent Ledger — donations + expenses  
4. Real-time balance tracking  
5. Proof Upload — bills/receipts for expenses  
6. Document Verification (concept)  
7. AI-based anomaly detection (concept)  
8. Public Trust Indicators — activity & transparency
